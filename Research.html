Overview

Research interests

My research concerns Poisson geometry, Dirac structures, symplectic and hyperkähler geometry, deformation theory, and moduli spaces. Two of my papers address these themes directly: From multiplicative to additive geometry: Deformation theory and 2D TQFT develops the Poisson-geometric passage between Hamiltonian and quasi-Hamiltonian structures, while Dirac geometry, deformation theory and shifted symplectic geometry develops the Dirac-geometric side.

A recurring question is how additive and multiplicative structures relate: Hamiltonian spaces carry Lie-algebra-valued moment maps, while quasi-Hamiltonian spaces carry group-valued moment maps. I study deformations between these settings and their behavior under fusion, reduction, and gluing.

Another part of my work builds geometric field theories from moduli spaces associated with quivers. My recent research develops a hyperkähler analogue using Nahm--Kirchhoff equations and analytic variational methods.

Nahm--Kirchhoff moduli spaces, hyperkähler quotient and 2D field theories

Mohamed Moussadek Maiza · Preprint, 2026

Preprint
arXiv:2610.03384PDF
Abstract

This work develops hyperkähler moduli spaces associated with Nahm--Kirchhoff equations on quivers and studies their two-dimensional field-theoretic structure. Analytic and variational methods prove a quiver analogue of a Donaldson-type theorem.

This work extends deformation methods to Dirac geometry and studies compatibility with reduction along strong Dirac maps, integration to quasi-symplectic groupoids, and Morita equivalence of Lie algebroids.

From multiplicative to additive geometry: Deformation theory and 2D TQFT

Mohamed Moussadek Maiza · Journal of Geometry and Physics (2026)

Published
JournalarXiv:2601.13455PDF
Abstract

This paper studies the Poisson-geometric transition between the multiplicative, group-valued setting of quasi-Hamiltonian spaces and the additive, Lie-algebra-valued setting of Hamiltonian spaces. It constructs deformations between these geometries and relates them to moduli spaces and two-dimensional field theories.

Lax--Kirchhoff moduli spaces and Hamiltonian 2D TQFT

Mohamed Moussadek Maiza and Maxence Mayrand · Geometriae Dedicata (2026)

Published
JournalarXiv:2510.23567PDF
Abstract

The paper constructs symplectic moduli spaces associated with Lax equations on quivers and Kirchhoff conditions at vertices. These spaces satisfy cutting and gluing laws and define a two-dimensional Hamiltonian TQFT.

Deformation spaces of quasi-Hamiltonian spaces

Jean-Philippe Burelle, Mohamed Moussadek Maiza, and Maxence Mayrand · Differential Geometry and its Applications (2026)

Published
JournalarXiv:2505.16689PDF
Abstract

This joint work constructs smooth deformations from quasi-Hamiltonian to Hamiltonian spaces, including deformations of the double, conjugacy classes near the identity, and moduli spaces of flat connections. Under suitable regularity assumptions, the construction is compatible with fusion and reduction.
